# AI Lab — Research State and Planning

Experiment results generated by the Blankdiss AI Lab.

Each experiment gets its own directory:

    data/ai_lab/results/<experiment-id>/

Typical output:

    results.json
    report.md

The results are committed back to the repository by GitHub Actions.

---

## Purpose

AI Lab is the reasoning and orchestration layer around the Blankdiss
research system.

It should not replace the Research Engine.

The intended separation is:

    Research Engine
        ↓
    reproducible research execution
        ↓
    machine-readable results
        ↓
    Research State
        ↓
    AI Lab Planner
        ↓
    next research proposal
        ↓
    controlled execution

AI Lab reasons about research state. It does not invent arbitrary
analysis code or silently modify research specifications.

---

## Research state

The research state is created by:

    ml/ai-lab/research_state.py

It provides an explicit, read-only snapshot of:

- current research specifications
- locked research specifications
- genuinely open research questions
- previous AI Lab experiments
- previous Research Engine runs

The state is versioned.

Current state version:

    state_version: 2

The snapshot is descriptive and auditable.

It does not:

- execute experiments
- modify research specifications
- modify research results
- select parameters
- optimize hypotheses
- declare a hypothesis confirmed

---

## Research-question classification

The research state distinguishes three different categories.

### 1. Open research questions

These are active research specifications that:

- have a research question
- are not locked
- are not marked as migration
- can therefore participate in the current research queue

They are exposed through:

    research.open_questions

Example:

    momentum_incremental_si

---

### 2. Locked research

Locked specifications are controlled checkpoints.

They remain visible through:

    research.locked_specs

A locked specification is not considered an open question.

This is important for prospective confirmation tests.

For example:

    momentum_si_prospective_confirmation

is explicitly locked.

The planner must not decide on its own that this confirmation test
is the next experiment.

A locked confirmation test requires an explicit execution decision.

---

### 3. Migration specifications

Specifications with:

    stage: migration

remain part of the complete specification inventory.

They are retained for traceability and migration work, but they are
not treated as current research evidence.

They are therefore excluded from:

    research.open_questions

and from automatic experiment selection.

## AI Lab planner

The planner is implemented in:

    ml/ai-lab/experiment_planner.py

The planner is deterministic and conservative.

Current planner version:

    PLANNER_VERSION: 2

The planner does not execute research.

It selects an existing eligible specification and creates a proposal
for controlled execution.

---

## Planner selection policy

The planner uses the following priority:

    1. unexecuted hypothesis tests
    2. unexecuted discovery / signal-mapping specifications

Within each category, specifications are considered in repository
order.

Locked specifications are never automatically selected.

Migration specifications are never automatically selected.

Already executed specifications are skipped when their specification
ID is present in a Research Engine run manifest.

This prevents the planner from repeatedly proposing the same research
specification.

---

## Current planning flow

The intended flow is:

    Research State
          │
          ├── open questions
          │
          ├── locked checkpoints
          │
          └── research runs
                  │
                  ▼
            AI Lab Planner
                  │
          ┌───────┴────────┐
          │                │
    eligible spec      no eligible spec
          │                │
          ▼                ▼
    proposal_created    no_proposal

The planner does not create a new research specification merely
because no existing specification is available.

If no eligible unexecuted hypothesis-test or discovery specification
exists, the planner returns:

    status: no_proposal

This is intentional.

---

## Locked confirmation boundary

A prospective confirmation test is different from discovery.

Discovery asks:

    What patterns are present?

Hypothesis testing asks:

    Does a predefined relationship hold?

Prospective confirmation asks:

    Does a previously fixed hypothesis survive on new,
    previously unused data?

Therefore a locked prospective confirmation must not be selected
automatically just because it exists.

The planner explicitly reports this condition as a warning.

Example:

    "Locked prospective-confirmation specification(s) exist but are
     not automatically selected by the planner."

This makes the boundary visible without allowing the planner to
silently promote a confirmation test into the research queue.

---

## Execution boundary

AI Lab planning and Research Engine execution are separate stages.

The planner may propose:

    momentum_incremental_si

but it does not execute the specification itself.

The intended future execution path is:

    planner
       ↓
    proposal
       ↓
    explicit/controlled execution
       ↓
    Research Engine
       ↓
    research run manifest
       ↓
    machine-readable result
       ↓
    next Research State snapshot

This separation is important for reproducibility and auditability.

---

## Already executed specifications

The planner reads:

    data/processed/ml/research/spec_runs/

and uses the specification IDs represented in run manifests to avoid
repeating completed research specifications.

This creates a simple research progression:

    unexecuted spec
        ↓
    planner proposal
        ↓
    research execution
        ↓
    run manifest
        ↓
    spec becomes completed
        ↓
    planner moves to next eligible spec

The planner therefore does not need to maintain its own mutable
"completed experiments" database.

The Research Engine run manifests remain the source of truth for
research execution history.

## AI Lab boundaries

AI Lab must not:

- modify research YAML specifications
- change locked parameters
- optimize thresholds on test data
- select test data based on outcomes
- switch the primary endpoint after seeing test results
- introduce arbitrary research parameters
- declare a hypothesis confirmed from planner output
- treat migration work as current evidence
- automatically promote a locked confirmation test

The purpose of AI Lab is to improve the research loop while keeping
the actual research decisions explicit, reproducible and auditable.

---

## Design principle

The core principle is:

    state → reasoning → controlled proposal → execution → result → state

not:

    AI → arbitrary code → arbitrary experiment → conclusion

The AI Lab should make the research process more systematic without
making it less controlled.
